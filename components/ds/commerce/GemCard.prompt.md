Encyclopaedia card for one gemstone variety.

```jsx
<GemCard name="Rubin" image={img} cuts={["Ungeschliffen", "Geschliffen", "Half & Half"]}
  description="Rubine stehen für Energie, Mut und Leidenschaft." />
```

The hue dot resolves from the German stone name against the `--gem-*` tokens; unknown names fall back to gold. Descriptions are one warm sentence, sentence case.
